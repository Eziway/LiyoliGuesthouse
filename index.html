Welcome to Liyoli Guest Houses

Where comfort meets tranquility in the heart of Mthatha.

Liyoli Guest Houses represents a premier hospitality brand owning and managing distinct guest house properties in prime locations across Mthatha, Eastern Cape. Our mission is to provide business travelers, vacationers, and families with superior comfort, ultimate privacy, and warm hospitality.

Each of our properties — Liyoli Guesthouse at Willow Drive and Liyoli Guesthouse at Delville Road — is uniquely designed to provide modern luxury, high-speed WiFi, self-catering options, and private secure parking, giving you a relaxing home away from home.

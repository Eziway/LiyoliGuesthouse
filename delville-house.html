Welcome to Liyoli Guesthouse at Delville Road

Liyoli Guesthouse at Delville Road is located at 31 Delville Rd, offering executive-level guest house accommodation in the heart of Mthatha.

Combining modern architecture with warm hospitality, Liyoli Guesthouse at Delville Road is tailored for guests seeking high privacy, comfort, and direct convenience to central Mthatha's commercial and travel hubs. Whether you're visiting for a business engagement or leisure getaway, our property provides a tranquil sanctuary.

Each room is fitted with sleek furnishings, high-speed WiFi, smart television entertainment, self-catering capabilities, and dedicated secure parking on site.

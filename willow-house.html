Welcome to Liyoli Guesthouse at Willow Drive

Liyoli Guesthouse at Willow Drive is Liyoli's flagship guest property, offering a tranquil suburban haven situated in Fort Gale, Mthatha.

Designed with meticulous attention to detail, Liyoli Guesthouse at Willow Drive caters to business executives, travelers, and holidaymakers seeking peace, privacy, and elegance. Guests can relax by our sparkling swimming pool, enjoy our lush gardens, or cook up a feast at our outdoor braai and self-catering kitchen.

Every room at Liyoli Guesthouse at Willow Drive is tastefully appointed with plush bedding, private en-suite bathrooms, high-speed WiFi, smart entertainment, and climate control to ensure an exceptional stay.

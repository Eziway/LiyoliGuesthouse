Comfortable & Fully Equipped Rooms

At Liyoli Guesthouse, each room is thoughtfully designed to provide guests with comfort and convenience during their stay. All guest rooms are equipped with a flat-screen TV with satellite channels, a microwave, and a kettle for preparing hot beverages. Guests can enjoy a modern walk-in shower, complimentary toiletries, and a spacious wardrobe for storage. Every room features a private bathroom and offers free Wi-Fi to keep you connected throughout your visit. Fresh bed linen and towels are provided to ensure a clean, relaxing, and enjoyable stay.
